Privacy Policy

JiraSync for Sheets, a Google Workspace add-on by Runneth Labs

This page is a general template provided for information. It is not legal advice. Last updated: October 28, 2026.

Summary

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not store your Jira data or your spreadsheet data on our servers.
  • Your Jira API token is stored only in your own Google account's user properties.
  • Jira data is written only to the Google Sheet you choose.

Information the add-on uses

  • Jira connection details. Your Jira site URL, account email and Jira API token. These are saved with Google Apps Script User Properties, which are private to your Google account and this add-on. Other users of the same spreadsheet cannot read them.
  • Jira data. When you run a sync, the add-on sends your JQL query to your Jira site over HTTPS and writes the returned issues and fields into the spreadsheet you have open. It does not copy this data anywhere else.
  • Spreadsheet access. The add-on reads and writes only the spreadsheet it is opened in, to create and update the sheets you sync into.
  • License key. If you buy a paid plan, the add-on sends your license key to Gumroad's license verification API to confirm it is valid. The key and its validation result may be cached in your user properties. Gumroad handles payments under its own privacy policy; we never see your card details.
  • Your email address. If the add-on requests it, your Google account email may be used to show which account is signed in and to send you sync-failure notices through your own Google account. It is not shared with third parties.

How information is shared

Data is sent only to the services needed to do the job: your Jira site (Atlassian), Google (to read and write your spreadsheet), and Gumroad (license checks only). We do not sell, rent or trade your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Google user data

JiraSync for Sheets' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not used to train AI or machine-learning models.

Retention and deletion

Because data lives in your Google account and your spreadsheet, you control it. To delete your stored Jira token and settings, use the add-on's disconnect option or remove the add-on from your Google account. Data written into your sheet stays there until you delete it. You can also revoke your Jira API token at any time in your Atlassian account settings.

Security

All requests to Jira, Google and Gumroad use HTTPS. We recommend creating a dedicated Jira API token for the add-on so you can revoke it independently.

Children

The add-on is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
